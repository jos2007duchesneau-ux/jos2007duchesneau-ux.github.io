Privacy policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Viral Clip Engine is a private, single-user tool used only by the owner of the StreamRush Clips YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Google user data accessed

  • YouTube upload (youtube.upload): to upload Shorts to the owner's own channel.
  • YouTube read-only (youtube.readonly): to confirm the connected channel and check the status of uploaded videos.
  • YouTube Analytics read-only (yt-analytics.readonly): to read the channel's own view and retention statistics.
  • Email address (openid, userinfo.email): to verify that the correct Google account is connected before uploading.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to publish and measure the owner's own Shorts. Access tokens and statistics are stored locally on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. No data is transferred to any server other than Google's.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and local data can be deleted by removing the tool's files.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
